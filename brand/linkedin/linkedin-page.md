### 1 — The launch post

> Isthmus Meridian is live.
>
> We run the back office behind private-market investors.
>
> Not software. Not a staffing agency. An embedded team that produces the analytical, operational, and reporting work an investment firm runs on — deal models, IC memos, diligence support, portfolio reporting, LP materials.
>
> The premise is simple. Deal volume and LP expectations have both risen. Team sizes have not. Most firms close that gap by hiring a function they will only fully use some of the time, or by buying a tool that someone internally then has to operate.
>
> We are the third option: you give us the work, we execute it.
>
> AI is how we deliver it faster and more consistently. It is not what we sell.
>
> isthmusmeridian.com

### 2 — The distinction post

> "Is this software?"
>
> It is the first question we get, and the answer matters.
>
> A tool gives your team a new thing to operate. It assumes you have the capacity to run it, the time to configure it, and someone who owns it when it breaks.
>
> We don't sell the tool. We run the work.
>
> The model produced, the memo written, the quarterly pack assembled — that is the deliverable. What we use to produce it is our problem, not yours.
>
> Not another tool for your team to manage. An embedded team that completes the work.

### 3 — The AI post

> Where AI actually helps in private markets — and where it doesn't.
>
> It helps with volume. Reviewing more documents than a person can read in the time available. Extracting and structuring data from inconsistent formats. Accelerating first-pass research. Producing recurring reports that follow the same shape every quarter.
>
> It does not replace judgement. It does not sign off an investment memo, and it does not decide what a number means.
>
> That is why every output we deliver is reviewed and delivered as a managed service. AI moves the floor, not the ceiling.
>
> Faster execution, without sacrificing institutional quality.

### 4 — The engagement-models post

> Three ways firms work with us.
>
> **Transaction Support** — you have a live deal and need capacity now. Screening, research, diligence, modeling, valuation, IC materials.
>
> **Embedded Back Office** — the standing operating layer. Deal support, financial analysis, investor materials, fund operations, portfolio monitoring, research, reporting, automation. This is where most firms land.
>
> **Portfolio and Fund Support** — you have the investment team, you need the recurring infrastructure. Portfolio reporting, KPI tracking, LP materials, fund documentation.
>
> Build the investment firm without building every function internally.

## Notes on what I did not write

**No client names, no metrics, no track record.** The findings doc references a
live growth-capital engagement but marks it *"subject to client disclosure
permissions."* Naming it, or quoting a number attached to it, is your call and
your client's — so the copy above claims capability, never results. Add proof
once you have permission in writing; it will be the strongest thing on the page.

**No employee count or founding date** — LinkedIn will ask; fill them in truthfully.
