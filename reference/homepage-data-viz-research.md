# Homepage Data Visualization Research

## Recommended approach

Use 2-3 simple charts on the homepage. The goal is not to make Isthmus look like analytics software. The charts should prove the market pressure behind the back-office offer:

1. Private-market work is getting more complex.
2. Reporting and data workflows are strained.
3. AI is being adopted, but firms still need managed execution and human oversight.

## Chart 1: Private-market pressure is rising

Place near the hero or immediately after the problem section.

Suggested chart type: simple horizontal bar chart.

Data points:

- Buyout and growth deals over $500M increased 44% in 2025.
- Overall PE deal value increased 17% in 2025.
- PE-backed exit value increased more than 40% in 2025.
- PE secondaries volume increased 48% in 2025.

Source: McKinsey, Global Private Equity Report 2026
https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/private-capital/our-insights/global-private-markets-report/private-equity

Message:

> More transaction activity, harder underwriting, and longer holding periods create more work for investment teams.

## Chart 2: The back office is the bottleneck

Place inside the "Running a fund requires more than an investment team" section.

Suggested chart type: problem stack or bar chart.

Data points:

- 89% of surveyed PE CFOs cited data fragmentation as their main reporting challenge.
- 58% of CFOs said LP requests seek more detail and analysis than in previous years.
- 44% of CFOs still rely on static reports with limited visualization.

Sources:

- Equiforte PE CFO reporting survey
  https://www.equiforte.com/resources/blog/pe-cfo-reporting-survey
- Maybern summary of Private Funds CFO Insights Survey 2025
  https://www.maybern.com/post/meeting-the-growing-demands-of-in-flight-lp-information-requests
- PwC CFO Compass Survey 2025
  https://www.pwc.com/mk/en/news/cfo-compass-survey-2025.html

Message:

> The pain is not lack of dashboards. The pain is fragmented work that needs to be completed, checked, and delivered.

## Chart 3: AI is active, but integration and execution matter

Place inside the "AI Advantage" section.

Suggested chart type: adoption gap chart.

Data points:

- 90% of M&A organizations currently use GenAI.
- 37% use GenAI across multiple M&A life-cycle stages.
- 35% realize the most value with advisory-led use.
- Only 5% of transaction-readiness respondents use AI-powered evaluations or data collection.

Sources:

- Deloitte 2026 Generative AI in M&A Pulse Study
  https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/what-we-do/capabilities/mergers-acquisitions-restructuring/articles/m-and-a-generative-ai-study.html
- Harvard Law School Forum, Transaction Readiness in Turbulent Times
  https://corpgov.law.harvard.edu/2025/11/04/ready-for-the-deal-transaction-readiness-in-turbulent-times/

Message:

> AI adoption is no longer novel, but useful AI still depends on integration, controls, and specialized operators.

## What not to chart

Avoid charts that make Isthmus look like a self-service investment intelligence platform:

- Generic market maps as the hero.
- Portfolio dashboards as the main product visual.
- Bloomberg-style terminal screenshots.
- Charts implying Isthmus makes investment decisions.

The charts should support the managed back-office positioning: "We run the work."
