Services

What Isthmus Meridian provides

The services are organized around the functions an investment firm needs to run.

Deal Team Support

Sourcing support, research, screening, diligence coordination, memos, and IC presentations.

Financial Modeling

Forecasting, valuation, comparable analysis, returns analysis, scenarios, and downside cases.

Capital Formation

Fund presentations, LP updates, DDQs, data-room materials, and investor meeting support.

Fund Operations

Pipeline administration, documentation, internal reporting, workflows, and operating calendars.

Portfolio Monitoring

KPI collection, financial analysis, budget-vs-actual tracking, dashboards, and board updates.

Research and Intelligence

Sector, company, transaction, geographic, regulatory, and thematic research.

Data and AI Enablement

Workflow mapping, document processing, reusable templates, automation, and custom tools.
