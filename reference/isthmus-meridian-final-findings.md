# Isthmus Meridian Final Findings

Correct. The website should position Isthmus Meridian as an **AI-enabled private-markets back-office provider**.

The investor is the customer, but the product is not primarily investment software or research. The product is:

> **A managed back office that executes the work investment firms need to operate.**

The deck states this clearly: **"We don't sell the tool. We run the work."** Isthmus operates from inside the fund's workflow and handles institutional materials, deal models, diligence, and portfolio materials.

## Correct positioning

### Primary message

> **We run the private-markets back office.**

### Supporting message

> Isthmus Meridian gives investment firms an integrated team for deal execution, financial analysis, investor materials, fund operations, portfolio reporting, research, data, and AI.

### AI message

> AI is how we deliver the work faster, more consistently, and at increasing scale.

Do not position AI as the service itself.

The investor should understand:

1. **You give us work.**
2. **We execute it.**
3. **We use AI and structured workflows to move faster.**
4. **You avoid hiring a large internal back-office team.**
5. **The service becomes more efficient as we learn your processes.**
